About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering is committed to providing a solid technical foundation and research capabilities in computing. We prepare professionals who can adapt to the rapidly changing technology landscape and contribute effectively to the software industry and society.

Vision

To evolve as a centre of academic excellence in Computer Science and Engineering by producing globally competent professionals with ethical values.

Mission

  • To provide high-quality technical education through modern teaching-learning practices.
  • To promote research, innovation, and entrepreneurship in computing domains.
  • To inculcate ethical behavior, leadership skills, and social responsibility.

Programs Offered

B.E. - Computer Science & Engineering
Undergraduate 4 Years

Eligibility Criteria

  • General Quota: Candidates should have passed the Higher Secondary Examination (10+2) or equivalent with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry.
  • Lateral Entry: Diploma holders in relevant branches of Engineering or B.Sc. in Mathematics are eligible for direct admission to the second year.
  • Admissions are based on the guidelines prescribed by the Directorate of Technical Education (DoTE), Tamil Nadu.

Software Engineering

Infrastructure & Facilities

The department features state-of-the-art laboratories with high-end workstations and the latest software development tools.

  • Advanced Programming Labs
  • Networking & Security Lab
  • Data Science & Big Data Center
  • Incubation & Innovation Hub

Student Opportunities

Students are encouraged to participate in national hackathons, open-source contributions, and industry internships. Our "Coding Club" and "CSI Student Chapter" organize regular tech talks and coding contests.
