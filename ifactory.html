Bridging Industry & Academia

The iFactory Network Lab at IGCE is a prestigious initiative by the Ministry of Heavy Industries (MHI), Govt. of India, implemented by C4i4 Lab, Pune. We demonstrate how Industry 4.0 technologies facilitate digital transformation in factories, education, and workforce development.

Supported By MHI, Govt. of India
Implemented By C4i4 Lab, Pune

Our Core Purpose

To create an experiential platform where students, MSMEs, and professionals can learn and apply Industry 4.0 technologies — driving innovation and sustainable growth.
